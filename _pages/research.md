<p class="lead">I work with global climate models to study the processes that shape clouds &mdash; in particular the role of ice-nucleating particles in mixed-phase clouds.</p>

<div class="prose" markdown="1">

My broader interests include high-resolution and large-eddy simulation approaches to convective processes; chemical processes in aerosols, and atmospheric gas-to-particle conversion; and vegetation’s influence on climate through aerosol–cloud interactions.

</div>
